# UNVERIFIED: phone and real glasses checks

No emulator was booted, no device was installed, and no visual result is claimed. Run these on a connected, API-36-capable phone and a compatible projected glasses display. Use the fleet manager's reviewed build; do not replace an accepted test artifact without its owner's authorization.

1. **Sample import:** In Author mode, create or open a sequenced actor mark. Choose **Pick from script…**. Under **Add a sample to this mark**, tap **The Last Work Light** once. Reopen the mark and verify eight ordered spoken cues were added and existing cues remain. Repeat on a fresh mark for each of the 11 samples. The horizontal sample strip scrolls. Import appends; tapping again adds another copy intentionally.
2. **No performer required:** Open the phone teleprompter before entering a spatial mark. Tap **Open on Glasses**. Single-line mode should preview the first spoken cue instead of Waiting. Use a blocking with no lines and confirm the glasses say **Open a script on your phone**.
3. **Cue and page transitions:** Import **Readable Under Pressure**. Play slowly, then drag through the long word and emoji lines. Verify every character remains readable, pages advance without skipping a word, the speaker label is bright, and the final display says **End of script**. Test at the device's largest font scale. The phone Size slider currently affects the phone only; projected sizes remain fixed.
4. **Flowing mode:** Switch from **line** to **scroll** beside the eye button. Import a long classic scene. Advance past several screenfuls and near the end. Verify the cyan line stays centered, the first and last spoken lines can reach center, and no last character remains cyan at progress 100%. Test both orientation and font-size changes on the phone.
5. **Timing and cancellation:** At 14 characters/second, run for 10 seconds; expect about 140 characters of cursor travel. Change speed several times. Pause for 30 seconds and confirm both displays freeze. Resume and confirm no cumulative speed-up. Close the phone dialog while running; the glasses should stop advancing. Reopen and record the resulting position; cross-dismissal persistence is not guaranteed.
6. **Mark following:** Drag within the script, then enter a different mark within three seconds. The manual position should be retained. After three seconds, enter another mark and confirm it follows. Walk or rotate while remaining on one mark: pose updates must not repeatedly reset reading. Rotate/recreate the phone at a saved cursor and verify the initial mark emission does not overwrite it.
7. **Phone controls and errors:** At a narrow portrait width, confirm Play, speed, Size, Voice, Auto-fire, Open on Glasses, and mode are reachable on wrapped rows. Deny microphone permission and verify the message explains that Play/drag still work. On a device without speech recognition, verify the unavailable message. Force a connection loss just before launch and verify the retry message if launch fails. An exception in connection observation still disables the button without a detailed error.
8. **Asset error:** In a disposable test build only, point a PlayRef at a nonexistent asset. Open it and verify **Could not load this script** with **Try again**, rather than an endless spinner. A failed sample chip should report failure and leave existing mark cues unchanged. Restore the valid asset reference afterward.
9. **Reconnect:** Pause on the phone, disconnect glasses, reconnect, and explicitly tap Open on Glasses. Verify the current document and position return; check whether the baseline launch flags create duplicate activities. Automatic reopen is deliberately not added. Repeat a disconnect while reading and record that the phone timer currently continues: automatic disconnect pause is unresolved.
10. **Process death and battery:** On a disposable session, stop the app through system settings and reopen from phone and from the glasses launcher. Record lost in-memory document/mode/cursor; durable glasses session recovery remains unresolved. Confirm the empty state gives a phone action. Leave the glasses idle and inspect screen/battery behavior; existing always-on/lock-screen flags remain. Close the projected activity through system controls after testing. Sustained speech-error retry/backoff and background-pause policy also remain unresolved.

The demo walkthrough has 369 words: about 2.84 minutes at 130 words/minute, plus pauses. Rehearse it with actual device transitions. Its readiness conditions are intentional; use current fleet verdicts and measured numbers when presenting, and skip unready items.
